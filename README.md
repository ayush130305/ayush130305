# Ayush Jain

Final-year Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering student (VLSI Honours) at DJSCE, Mumbai. I design and verify digital hardware: RTL, FPGA and a little silicon. FPGA Developer Intern at Vicharak.

[Portfolio](https://ayush130305.github.io)

## Featured projects

- **[4-bit Accumulator CPU](https://github.com/ayush130305/4-bit-Accumulator-CPU)**: taped out on SCL's 1.2 µm open PDK. Timing signed off at 10 MHz, gate-level and post-layout simulation pass, LVS match, DRC clean.
- **[AXI-QSPI Controller](https://github.com/ayush130305/AXI-QSPI-Controller-with-AXI-Wrapper)**: QSPI flash controller with an AXI4-Lite register interface and a memory-mapped XIP window. Demonstrated [booting a PicoRV32 from external flash on a Basys 3](https://github.com/ayush130305/PicoRV32-XIP-Boot-Demonstration-Basys-3).
- **[TinyML-fpga](https://github.com/ayush130305/TinyML-fpga)**: run int8 TensorFlow Lite models on any FPGA, bit-exact with LiteRT.
- **[UART to I2C/SPI Bridge](https://github.com/ayush130305/UART_I2C_SPI_bridge)**: control SPI and I2C peripherals from a PC over UART. Verified with cocotb and on hardware.
- **[19-bit Harvard CPU](https://github.com/ayush130305/19bit_cpu_vicharak)**: 5-stage pipeline with hazard handling, FFT and XOR-encryption units.
